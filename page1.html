My Top 5 WNBA Players of All Time

These are my personal top five WNBA players of all time. I chose these players because of their talent, awards, championships, and impact on women's basketball.

1. Diana Taurasi

Diana Taurasi is one of the greatest scorers in WNBA history. She played for the Phoenix Mercury and won three WNBA championships. Her scoring ability and long career make her my number one player.

2. Lisa Leslie

Lisa Leslie was one of the most dominant centers in WNBA history. She won two WNBA championships and three MVP awards. She helped make women's basketball more popular.

3. Sue Bird

Sue Bird was an amazing point guard who played for the Seattle Storm. She won four WNBA championships and was known for her leadership, passing, and ability to make her teammates better.

4. Cynthia Cooper

Cynthia Cooper was a major part of the Houston Comets dynasty. She won four WNBA championships and two MVP awards. She was one of the league's first great stars.

5. Maya Moore

Maya Moore was one of the best players of her generation. She won four WNBA championships with the Minnesota Lynx and was known for her scoring and all-around basketball skills.
